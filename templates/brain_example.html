AI Detection Capabilities

Our AI model segments and detects various brain tumor types in MRI scans

Gliomas

Includes astrocytomas, oligodendrogliomas, and glioblastomas. Most common primary brain tumors, often malignant.

Meningiomas

Tumors arising from the membranes surrounding the brain and spinal cord. Usually benign but can be aggressive.

Pituitary Adenomas

Tumors developing in the pituitary gland, affecting hormone production and neurological function.

Metastatic Tumors

Cancers that have spread to the brain from other parts of the body, often requiring complex treatment.
